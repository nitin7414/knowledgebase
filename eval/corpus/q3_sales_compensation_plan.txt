# Q3 2026 Enterprise Sales Compensation & Commission Policy (Distractor)

## Section 1: Regional Quota Targets & Baseline Commission
The Q3 2026 sales commission structure incentivizes annual multi-workspace enterprise contract expansions:
1. North America Enterprise Territory: Baseline quota of $2.2M per Account Executive. Baseline commission rate is 10.5% on recognized net new annual contract value (ACV).
2. EMEA Enterprise Territory: Baseline quota of €1.8M per AE. Baseline commission rate is 11.0% on net recognized revenue.
3. APAC Regional Territory: Fastest accelerating market with a baseline quota of $1.2M per AE and a 12.0% commission baseline.

## Section 2: Quota Accelerators & Multi-Year Incentives
Account Executives who exceed 100% of quarterly quota unlock progressive commission accelerators:
- 101% to 125% of Quota: 1.5x commission multiplier applied to all revenue booked beyond quota target.
- 126% to 150% of Quota: 2.0x commission multiplier applied to incremental bookings.
- Contracts signed with upfront 3-year commitments receive an additional 3.0% margin kicker bonus.

## Section 3: Clawback & Revenue Recognition Timing
Commissions are distributed within two payroll cycles following customer invoice payment.
If an enterprise subscription contract churns or terminates within the first 90 days, 100% of the commission paid is subject to unearned clawback deductions from future cycles.
