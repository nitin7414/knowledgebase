# Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment & Data Security Policy (Distractor)

## Section 1: Vendor Security Tiers & Certification Requirements
All third-party SaaS vendors and sub-processors handling confidential company data must complete our annual vendor security assessment.
Vendors are classified into three risk tiers:
- Critical Tier: Vendors hosting production customer databases or processing vector embeddings. They must provide an audited SOC 2 Type II report and ISO/IEC 27001 certification within the last 12 months.
- Elevated Tier: Vendors providing development tools, CI/CD runners, or operational logging platforms.
- Standard Tier: Administrative SaaS applications with no access to customer confidential data.

## Section 2: Encryption & Data Sovereignty Mandates
Third-party systems must enforce AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all uploaded payloads.
Under no circumstances may vendors use customer data to train external proprietary foundation models without explicit contractual consent.
Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) are mandatory for any enterprise sub-processor handling sensitive financial audit records.
All contracts must execute a standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) incorporating EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

## Section 3: Vendor Breach Notification & Termination
In the event of a suspected security compromise, vendors are contractually bound to deliver formal written notice to our Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) within 24 hours of discovery.
Failure to deliver timely breach disclosure constitutes an immediate cause for contract termination without financial penalty.
